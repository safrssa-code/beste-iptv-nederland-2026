IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) has become a serious alternative to expensive TV subscriptions. With a single app you get access to thousands of channels, movies, series and all sport, without a dish and without a contract.

What is IPTV?

With IPTV, television is delivered over the internet instead of cable or satellite. You watch on your Smart TV, phone, tablet, Firestick or Android box. A good service offers 32,000+ live channels and 160,000 movies & series, often in 4K/FHD.

Why choose IPTV in 2026?

How to choose the right IPTV provider?

  1. Quality: go for 4K/FHD without buffering, with stable EU servers.
  2. Channels: check that your channels and sport are included.
  3. Price and term: compare 6, 12 and 24 months.
  4. Payment: iDEAL, Bancontact, card or PayPal.
  5. Support and guarantee: 24/7 help and a 30-day money-back guarantee.
